# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the Quantova community to be a place where anyone can take part without fear of harassment. This applies to every space the project runs, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel connected to the organization.

## Expected behavior

* Treat other people with respect
* Assume good intent and give helpful, specific feedback
* Keep conversation focused on the work and the technology
* Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind
* Personal or political attacks
* Publishing another person's private information without consent
* Sustained disruption of discussion

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainers privately through the organization. Reports are handled discreetly. Please include what happened, where it happened, and any context that helps us understand the situation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove content or restrict participation for anyone who breaks them. Decisions aim to be fair and proportionate.

## Scope

This code applies within all project spaces and also when someone represents the project in a public space.
